# Aurora Labs Employee Handbook (Version 2)

*Effective: 2025-01-01 — this version supersedes Version 1.*

Welcome to Aurora Labs. This handbook summarizes the policies that apply to
all employees.

## Work schedule

Aurora Labs is a remote-first company. Core collaboration hours are 10:00
to 15:00 Pacific, and employees are expected to overlap with their team
during those hours. Flexible schedules are allowed with manager approval.

## Paid time off

**PTO: 20 days per year (updated 2025-01).** Employees accrue 20 paid time
off days per calendar year, prorated by start date. PTO carries over up to
10 days into the next year. Unused days beyond the carryover limit are
forfeited. Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance when
possible.

## Sick leave

**Sick leave: unlimited (updated 2025-01).** Version 2 replaces the fixed
5-day sick leave allowance from Version 1 with an unlimited, trust-based
sick leave policy. Employees take what they need for personal illness,
medical appointments, or caring for an ill dependent, and are expected to
communicate with their manager as soon as they know they will be out. There
is no accrual, no cap, and no carryover to track.

## Probation period

New hires serve a 90-day probation period. During probation, either party
may end employment with 7 days notice. Performance is reviewed at the
60-day mark.

## Equity

All full-time employees receive stock options with a 4-year vesting
schedule and a 1-year cliff. Options vest monthly after the cliff. Version 2
adds an annual equity refresher review for all employees who have been with
the company more than 12 months, awarded as part of the annual performance
review.

## Expenses

Reasonable work-from-home expenses up to $750 per year (updated 2025-01,
previously $500) are reimbursed without prior approval. Larger purchases
require manager sign-off.

## Parental leave

New parents are eligible for 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, with an
additional 4 weeks for the birthing parent. Leave may be taken within the
first 12 months of the child's arrival.

## Sabbatical

**Sabbatical: 4 weeks after 5 years (new in Version 2).** Employees who have
completed 5 continuous years of service are eligible for a 4-week fully
paid sabbatical. Sabbaticals must be approved by your manager and People
Operations, and are scheduled at least 3 months in advance. The sabbatical
is in addition to PTO and does not reduce the following year's PTO accrual.

## Holidays

Aurora Labs observes 11 paid company holidays per year, including New Year's
Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and the Friday
after, and the Christmas through New Year break. The full holiday calendar
is published each December for the following year.

## Remote work and travel

Aurora Labs is remote-first, but employees must be tax-compliant in the
location where they work. Working from a location other than your approved
home office for more than 30 consecutive days requires advance approval.
Business travel is covered by the travel policy: flights booked at least
14 days in advance, accommodation within the published per-diem limits, and
meals reimbursed up to $75 per day.

## Performance reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year: a mid-year check-in and an annual
review. The annual review determines merit increases and equity refreshes.
Reviews are based on written feedback from your manager, peers, and direct
reports, and you can add a self-assessment. In Version 2, employees can
submit feedback on managers through the same anonymous tool used for peer
feedback, and the People team reviews manager feedback trends quarterly.

## Learning and development

Each employee receives an annual learning budget of $1,000 for courses,
conferences, and books. Amounts under $500 are approved automatically;
larger requests go to your manager. Time spent on job-related learning
during working hours is encouraged.

## Wellness

Version 2 introduces a $50 per month wellness stipend for gym memberships,
fitness classes, or mental health apps, in addition to the benefits in the
benefits FAQ. The stipend is paid through payroll and does not require
receipts.

## Code of conduct

We expect everyone to treat colleagues with respect. Harassment of any kind
is prohibited and may result in termination.

## Conflict of interest

Employees must avoid activities that conflict with the company's interests.
Accepting gifts, payments, or favors from vendors, customers, or
competitors is prohibited without prior approval. Side projects are allowed
provided they do not compete with Aurora Labs, do not use company
resources, and do not interfere with your responsibilities.

## Termination and notice

Voluntary resignation requires two weeks written notice, during which the
employee is expected to hand off their work. Involuntary termination
follows the process set out in your employment agreement and applicable
local law. On termination, company equipment is returned within 14 days
and access to company systems is revoked on the last working day. In
Version 2, departing employees receive an offboarding checklist covering
benefits continuation (COBRA or local equivalent), equity exercise windows,
and the return of company property.

## Security and acceptable use

Every employee is responsible for the security of company systems. Use
unique passwords and enable multi-factor authentication on your company
accounts. Company devices must be encrypted at rest and locked when
unattended. Do not install unapproved software, and report suspected
phishing or security incidents immediately to security@auroralabs.example.
Version 2 requires all employees to complete the annual security training
module before the end of the first quarter, and new hires complete it
during onboarding week.

## Internal communication

The company communicates through Slack and email. The #announcements
channel is the authoritative source for company-wide updates. Meetings
should have a written agenda when they involve more than three people, and
decisions are recorded in the shared wiki. In Version 2, the monthly
all-hands includes a 15-minute open Q&A where any employee can submit
questions anonymously in advance, and the leadership team publishes the
answers in the wiki the same day.

## Time tracking and attendance

Salaried employees are not required to track their hours; work is judged
by outcomes rather than time at the desk. Employees on hourly or
contractor arrangements should log hours in the time-tracking tool each
week. Planned absences longer than 3 days are entered in the shared
calendar. With the unlimited sick leave policy in Version 2, there is no
accrual to track for illness, and managers are asked not to require medical
certificates for absences of 3 days or fewer.

## Equipment and asset management

New hires receive a company laptop, monitor, and headset within the first
week. Equipment is the property of the company and must be returned within
14 days of termination. Lost or damaged equipment is reported to IT, and
replacement is provided for work-related loss. Software licenses are
managed centrally: request new tools through the IT portal rather than
purchasing them individually.
