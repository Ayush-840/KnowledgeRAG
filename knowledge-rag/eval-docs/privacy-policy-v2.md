# Aurora Labs Privacy Policy (Version 2)

*Effective: 2025-01-15 — this version supersedes Version 1.*

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Aurora Labs Inc. ("Aurora Labs", "we", "us")
collects, uses, and protects personal data when you use our analytics,
alerting, and data pipeline products, including Atlas, Beacon, and Nimbus.

## 2. Data controller

The data controller for all personal data processed under this policy is
Aurora Labs Inc., 415 Mission Street, San Francisco, CA 94105. Questions about
this policy can be sent to privacy@auroralabs.example.

## 3. Data we collect

We collect account information (name, work email, company), usage telemetry
(login events, query counts, feature usage), and any content you upload to
your workspace. We do not sell personal data.

## 4. Lawful basis

We process personal data on the basis of contract performance, legitimate
interest, and consent where required. Where consent is the basis, you may
withdraw it at any time through the product settings.

## 5. Data retention

**Retention period: 24 months (updated 2025-01).** Customer content is
retained for twenty-four months after the end of the subscription term, after
which it is permanently deleted from our systems. Usage telemetry is retained
for 12 months. Account records are retained for as long as your account is
active.

## 6. Data subject rights

Data subjects may request access, correction, deletion, or portability of
their personal data by emailing privacy@auroralabs.example. We respond to
verified requests within 30 days.

## 7. International transfers

Personal data may be transferred to subprocessors located in the United
States and the European Union. Transfers outside the EEA are governed by
Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 8. Security

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, including
AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.2+ in transit.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We will notify customers by email at least 30 days before a material change
to this policy takes effect.
