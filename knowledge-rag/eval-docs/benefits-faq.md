# Aurora Labs Benefits FAQ

Answers to common questions about employee benefits at Aurora Labs.

## Health insurance

Aurora Labs offers medical, dental, and vision insurance through two
providers: Aetna and Kaiser. The company pays 100% of employee premiums and
80% of dependent premiums. Coverage starts on the first day of the month
after your start date.

## 401(k)

The company offers a 401(k) plan through Fidelity with a 4% match on a
dollar-for-dollar basis, fully vested from day one. You can enroll at any
time through the Fidelity portal.

## Equity

Every full-time employee receives stock options. The standard grant vests
over 4 years with a 1-year cliff. See the employee handbook for details.

## Parental leave

New parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, plus 4 additional
weeks for the birthing parent. See employee-handbook-v2 for the current
policy.

## Learning budget

Each employee gets an annual learning budget of $1,000 for courses,
conferences, and books. Approval is automatic for amounts under $500.

## Wellness

All employees get a $50 per month wellness stipend for gym memberships,
fitness classes, or mental health apps.

## When do benefits start?

Most benefits start on the first day of the month following your start date.
401(k) matching starts immediately with your first paycheck.

## What health plans are available?

Two networks: Aetna (PPO, nationwide) and Kaiser (HMO, available in
California, Colorado, Georgia, Hawaii, Maryland, Oregon, Virginia, and
Washington). You choose one at enrollment and can switch during open
enrollment each November. Telehealth visits through the plan's virtual care
provider are covered at no copay.

## Do you offer an HSA or FSA?

Yes. Employees on the high-deductible health plan can open a Health Savings
Account (HSA) through our administrator, with a company contribution of
$500 per year for individuals and $1,000 for families. A flexible spending
account (FSA) for medical and dependent care is available to all employees
regardless of plan, with a $3,050 annual limit for medical and $5,000 for
dependent care.

## Is there disability insurance?

Yes. Aurora Labs provides short-term disability coverage at 60% of salary
for up to 12 weeks and long-term disability at 60% of salary until age 65,
both fully paid by the company. Short-term disability runs concurrently
with the state program where one applies.

## What about life insurance?

All employees receive a basic life insurance policy of $50,000 and an
accidental death and dismemberment policy of $50,000, both company-paid.
You can purchase supplemental life insurance for yourself (up to 5x
salary) and dependents through the enrollment portal.

## Does Aurora Labs offer mental health support?

Yes. The Employee Assistance Program (EAP) provides up to 6 free counseling
sessions per issue, per year, through a confidential third-party provider.
In addition, the health plans include behavioral health coverage, and the
wellness stipend can be used for mental health apps such as meditation and
therapy platforms.

## Is there a commuter benefit?

Employees who work in the office receive a pre-tax commuter benefit of up
to $150 per month for transit or parking, set up through payroll. Remote
employees can redirect the same amount to the wellness stipend instead.

## What about fertility and family-forming benefits?

The health plans include coverage for fertility diagnostics and treatment
subject to plan terms. Aurora Labs additionally reimburses up to $10,000
per calendar year for adoption or surrogacy expenses, and up to $5,000 for
egg or sperm freezing, through the benefits portal.

## How does open enrollment work?

Open enrollment runs every November for coverage starting January 1. You
can change plans, add dependents, or adjust HSA and FSA contributions
during this window. Outside open enrollment, changes are allowed only for
qualifying life events (marriage, birth, loss of other coverage) within
30 days of the event.

## What happens to benefits when I leave?

Health, dental, and vision coverage continues through the end of the month
in which you leave, after which COBRA continuation is available at your own
cost for up to 18 months. Unused FSA funds are forfeited unless you elect
COBRA for the FSA. Stock options remain exercisable for 90 days after
termination, or longer if your grant agreement provides for an extended
window.

## Where can I find plan documents?

All plan documents — summaries of benefits and coverage (SBCs), the 401(k)
summary plan description, and the EAP brochure — are available in the
benefits portal under Documents. The People team can also provide printed
copies on request. Questions about a specific claim should go to the plan
provider's customer service line first, then to People Operations if
unresolved.

## Can I add or remove dependents mid-year?

Yes. Adding a dependent (marriage, birth, adoption) or losing coverage
elsewhere are qualifying life events that allow changes outside open
enrollment, provided you enroll or change within 30 days of the event.
Documentation such as a marriage certificate or birth record is required
for dependent additions. Removing a dependent who gains other coverage
can be done at any time through the benefits portal.

## Is dental and vision coverage separate from medical?

Dental and vision are included in the same enrollment election as medical
and are offered through the same carriers. There is no separate premium
for employees — the company pays 100% of employee dental and vision
premiums as well. Orthodontia for dependents is covered up to $1,500 per
lifetime, and vision covers one eye exam and one pair of glasses or
contacts per year.

## What should I do if my provider is out of network?

Out-of-network coverage is available on the Aetna PPO at a higher
cost-sharing level; the Kaiser HMO does not cover out-of-network care
except for emergencies. If you are considering a provider, check the
plan's directory before scheduling. If a medically necessary provider is
out of network and no in-network alternative exists, you can request a
single-case agreement through the plan administrator, which may cover the
care at the in-network rate.

## Are part-time employees eligible for benefits?

Employees regularly scheduled for 30 or more hours per week receive the
full benefits package. Employees scheduled between 20 and 29 hours
receive medical, dental, and vision coverage with the company paying 50%
of employee premiums, and are not eligible for the 401(k) match or the
wellness stipend. Employees below 20 hours are not benefit-eligible but
may purchase coverage through the marketplace. Eligibility is re-evaluated
at each open enrollment.

## How do I use the wellness stipend?

Submit a receipt through the benefits portal for gym memberships, fitness
classes, or mental health apps, and the amount is added to your next
paycheck. The stipend is $50 per month and does not roll over. Receipts
must be dated within the month claimed. If you work in an office, you can
alternatively use the commuter benefit of up to $150 per month in place
of the wellness stipend, but not both.
