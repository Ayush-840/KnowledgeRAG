# Aurora Labs Privacy Policy (Version 2)

*Effective: 2025-01-15 — this version supersedes Version 1.*

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Aurora Labs Inc. ("Aurora Labs", "we", "us")
collects, uses, and protects personal data when you use our analytics,
alerting, and data pipeline products, including Atlas, Beacon, and Nimbus.

This policy applies to the Aurora Labs cloud platform, the web application at
app.auroralabs.example, our mobile companion apps, and the APIs used by our
developer products. It does not apply to third-party services that you connect
to your workspace. If you are an employee, contractor, or job applicant, see
the separate Employee Privacy Notice.

## 2. Data controller

The data controller for all personal data processed under this policy is
Aurora Labs Inc., 415 Mission Street, San Francisco, CA 94105. Questions about
this policy can be sent to privacy@auroralabs.example. Aurora Labs has also
appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO), who can be reached at
dpo@auroralabs.example, in accordance with Article 37 of the GDPR.

## 3. Data we collect

We collect account information (name, work email, company), usage telemetry
(login events, query counts, feature usage), and any content you upload to
your workspace. We do not sell personal data.

The categories of personal data we process include:

- **Account data.** Name, work email address, company name, billing address,
  and payment information. Payment card numbers are processed by our payment
  processor and are never stored on our servers.
- **Usage data.** Login timestamps, IP address, browser and device type, pages
  visited, feature usage, query counts, and error logs.
- **Workspace content.** Documents, notes, dashboards, alert configurations,
  and any other content that account holders upload, create, or share within a
  workspace.
- **Communications.** Emails and support tickets you send to our support team.
- **Event data.** Webhook payloads and integration configuration data.

We collect personal data directly from you, from your organization when an
administrator provisions your account, and automatically through our systems
when you use the platform.

## 4. Lawful basis

We process personal data on the basis of contract performance, legitimate
interest, and consent where required. Where consent is the basis, you may
withdraw it at any time through the product settings.

Our legitimate interests include operating and securing the platform,
preventing fraud and abuse, understanding product usage, and communicating
with customers about the services they have purchased. Where we rely on
legitimate interest, we document the balancing assessment in our privacy
records.

## 5. How we use personal data

We use personal data for the following purposes:

1. To provide, maintain, and operate the products you subscribe to.
2. To bill for the services and manage subscriptions and renewals.
3. To provide customer support and respond to inquiries.
4. To monitor the security and integrity of the platform and investigate
   incidents, fraud, or abuse.
5. To improve our products through aggregated and de-identified usage
   analytics.
6. To send service notifications, such as planned maintenance, security
   advisories, and billing reminders.
7. To send marketing communications, but only where you have not opted out or
   where consent is required and has been obtained.

**AI features and model training (new in Version 2).** Where you enable
AI-assisted features such as query summarization and document insights, we
process your workspace content to generate the output you request. Version 2
introduces a **model training opt-out**: by default, workspace content is not
used to train our models. If we later intend to use content for training, we
will ask for separate consent and you may withdraw it at any time. Version 1
contained no such commitment, which is why this clause was added.

## 6. Cookies and similar technologies

Our web application uses cookies and local storage to keep you signed in, to
remember your preferences, and to measure feature usage. The cookies we use
are:

- **Essential cookies.** Required for authentication and security. These
  cannot be disabled.
- **Preference cookies.** Remember settings such as timezone, dashboard
  density, and notification preferences.
- **Analytics cookies.** Measure how the application is used. In Version 2,
  analytics cookies are **disabled by default** and are only set after you
  accept them through the cookie banner — a change from Version 1, where they
  were on by default with an opt-out.

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers in the product.

## 7. How we share personal data

We share personal data only as needed to operate the service:

- **Subprocessors.** We share data with subprocessors that provide cloud
  infrastructure, email delivery, and customer support tooling. A current list
  is available at privacy.auroralabs.example/subprocessors.
- **Payment processor.** Your payment information is shared with our payment
  processor to complete transactions.
- **Professional advisers.** We may share data with legal, accounting, and
  security advisers where necessary.
- **Authorities.** We disclose data to law enforcement or regulators only
  where required by law and, where permitted, we notify the account holder
  before doing so.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data, and we do not share personal
data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

## 8. Data retention

**Retention period: 24 months (updated 2025-01).** Customer content is
retained for twenty-four months after the end of the subscription term, after
which it is permanently deleted from our systems. Usage telemetry is retained
for 12 months. Account records are retained for as long as your account is
active.

Backup copies of customer content are retained for no longer than 30 days
after deletion from the primary systems. Where a legal hold applies, data is
retained until the hold is lifted. The shortened retention periods in Version
2 (24 months for content, 12 months for telemetry, versus 36 and 24 in
Version 1) reflect our data minimization commitments.

## 9. Data subject rights

Data subjects may request access, correction, deletion, or portability of
their personal data by emailing privacy@auroralabs.example. We respond to
verified requests within 30 days.

We verify the identity of the requester before acting on a request. Where a
request concerns data for which a customer is the controller, we coordinate
with that customer to fulfil the request. In Version 2, deletion requests can
also be filed through the admin console under Settings > Privacy > Data
subject requests, which routes the request directly to our privacy team.

## 10. International transfers

Personal data may be transferred to subprocessors located in the United
States and the European Union. Transfers outside the EEA are governed by
Standard Contractual Clauses.

For transfers of personal data from the EEA to the United States, Aurora Labs
relies on EU Standard Contractual Clauses (2021/914) together with a transfer
impact assessment (TIA) that evaluates the laws of the destination country and
the supplementary measures we apply, including encryption in transit and at
rest and contractual restrictions on onward transfer.

## 11. Security

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, including
AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.2+ in transit.

Additional controls include: role-based access control for our staff, enforced
multi-factor authentication for administrative accounts, quarterly
penetration testing by an independent firm, and a vendor security review
program for subprocessors. Access to production systems is logged and
reviewed. In the event of a security incident affecting personal data, we
notify affected customers and, where required, the relevant supervisory
authority, without undue delay and no later than 72 hours after we become
aware of the incident.

## 12. Children's privacy

Our products are business tools and are not directed at children under the age
of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe
a child has provided us with personal data, contact privacy@auroralabs.example
and we will delete it.

## 13. Marketing communications

We send product updates and occasional newsletters to work email addresses.
You can opt out of marketing email at any time using the unsubscribe link in
each message or by updating your notification preferences. Opting out of
marketing does not stop service notifications.

## 14. Third-party links and integrations

The platform may contain links to third-party websites and may connect to
third-party services through integrations. We are not responsible for the
privacy practices of those third parties. When you connect an integration, the
integration provider's own terms apply to the data it receives.

## 15. Automated decision-making

We do not use personal data for automated decision-making that produces legal
or similarly significant effects on individuals. Alerting features in Beacon
may rank notifications by relevance, but these rankings are based on account
configuration and do not constitute automated decision-making as defined by
applicable law.

## 16. Employee privacy notice

If you apply for a role at Aurora Labs or join us as an employee, the
Employee Privacy Notice applies. It describes the categories of employment
data we process, retention of records, and your rights.

## 17. Complaints and supervisory authority

You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority at any
time. We ask that you first contact us at privacy@auroralabs.example so we can
attempt to resolve your concern, but you are not required to do so before
contacting a supervisory authority.

## 18. Changes to this policy

We will notify customers by email at least 30 days before a material change
to this policy takes effect.

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date at the top
of the policy. Version 2's 30-day notice period is longer than the 14-day
period in Version 1, giving customers more time to review changes that affect
their data.

## 19. Data processing for support requests

When you open a support ticket, our support team may access your workspace to
reproduce and investigate the issue. Support access is logged, limited to what
is needed to resolve the ticket, and reviewed periodically. In Version 2,
workspace owners are notified by email when a support agent accesses their
workspace, and the access is recorded in the workspace's audit log. Support
agents cannot access workspaces that have been marked "high privacy" by the
administrator, which requires the customer to grant access explicitly per
ticket.

## 20. Data in shared workspaces

When a workspace is shared between multiple account holders, each member can
see the content uploaded to that workspace, subject to the access controls the
administrator configures. Members who leave the workspace retain access until
the administrator removes them. In Version 2, a removed member's telemetry is
deleting 90 days after removal unless the workspace administrator requests
earlier deletion, and the deletion is confirmed in the audit log.

## 21. Aggregated analytics

We use aggregated and de-identified usage analytics to understand product
usage, improve performance, and publish aggregate benchmarks. Aggregates are
designed so that no individual or organization can be identified from them.
In Version 2, customers on Enterprise plans can opt out of aggregate
benchmark publication through the admin console, which excludes their
workspace's data from the published figures while retaining internal
aggregation for service quality.

## 22. Contractual commitments to customers

Version 2 of this policy is incorporated by reference into the Master
Services Agreement and the DPA. Customers on Enterprise plans may request a
signed copy of this policy for their records, and the policy version in
effect at the time of the subscription is the one that governs. Where this
policy conflicts with an older version of the agreement, the terms that
provide greater protection to data subjects prevail.
