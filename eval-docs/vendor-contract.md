# Master Services Agreement — Acme Corp (v1)

*Agreement ID: MSA-2023-0042. Signed 2023-04-10.*

## Parties

This Master Services Agreement is between Acme Corp ("Customer") and Aurora
Labs Inc. ("Provider") for the provision of the Atlas analytics platform.

## Services

Provider will make available the Atlas analytics platform, including
dashboards, SQL querying, and scheduled report delivery, as described in the
applicable service description. Access is granted to up to 50 named users.

The Services are provided as a hosted, software-as-a-service offering.
Customer may add named users up to the licensed limit without additional
documentation; exceeding the limit requires an order form amendment.
Provider may make changes to the Services during the term, provided the
changes do not materially reduce the functionality described in the
service description.

## Fees and payment

The annual fee is $48,000, payable quarterly in advance. Fees exclude
applicable taxes. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

Invoices are issued on the first day of each quarter. Customer will pay
within 30 days of the invoice date. Provider may withhold new user
provisioning while an invoice is more than 15 days past due, and may
suspend the Services entirely if payment is more than 30 days past due.
Fees are non-cancellable for the current term and non-refundable except as
set out in the termination section.

## Term and renewal

**Initial term: 12 months.** The agreement renews automatically for
successive 12-month terms unless either party gives written notice of
non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the then-current term.

## Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach with 30 days written notice
and an opportunity to cure. Provider may suspend service for non-payment
after 15 days past due.

Either party may terminate immediately if the other becomes insolvent or
ceases operations. Upon termination for convenience (not available in the
initial term), Customer receives a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees for the
unused portion of the term. Upon termination for Provider's uncured breach,
Customer receives a refund of prepaid fees and the remedies in the
limitation of liability section apply.

## Limitation of liability

Each party's aggregate liability is limited to the fees paid during the
12 months preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or
consequential damages.

"Consequential damages" includes loss of profits, loss of data, loss of
goodwill, and business interruption, whether foreseeable or not. This
limitation applies regardless of the theory of liability. The liability
cap is calculated from the fees actually paid in the 12 months before the
event giving rise to the claim, not the committed contract value.

## Data protection

The parties will execute a DPA governing personal data processed under
this agreement. Provider processes Customer Content solely to provide the
Services.

Customer retains all rights in Customer Content. Provider acquires no
rights in Customer Content other than the limited right to process it to
provide the Services and to improve the platform in aggregated,
de-identified form. In version 1 of this agreement, provider use of
de-identified data for product improvement does not require additional
customer consent.

## Service availability

Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain availability
of the Services. Version 1 does not include a contractual uptime guarantee
with credits; the parties agree to review availability quarterly against
the metrics in the service description. If measured availability falls
below 99% in any calendar quarter, Provider will provide a 5% credit
against the next quarterly invoice as a good-faith remedy, without
establishing a recurring commitment.

## Support

Provider will provide support during business hours (09:00-17:00 Pacific,
Monday through Friday) via the support portal. The initial response target
for reported service issues is 4 business hours. Support is provided in
English. Customer's primary and secondary contacts are set out in the order
form; changes to contacts are made through the portal.

## Confidentiality

Each party will protect the other's Confidential Information using the
same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and in
no event less than reasonable care. Confidential Information includes
Customer Content, pricing, and the terms of this agreement. The
confidentiality obligations survive for 3 years after termination, and
for 5 years for trade secrets.

## Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California.

The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal
courts in San Francisco County, California. This agreement is the entire
agreement between the parties and supersedes prior written or oral
agreements. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.

## Ordering and users

The Services are ordered through order forms issued under this agreement.
Each order form identifies the tier, the number of named users, the fee,
and the service term. Version 1 licenses 50 named users; additional users
are added by order form at the then-current per-user rate. Named users are
individuals, not shared accounts, and the Customer is responsible for the
accuracy of its user list. User seats may be reassigned to new individuals
within the licensed limit without an order form change.

## Customer obligations

Customer is responsible for the accounts provisioned under the agreement,
including maintaining accurate user lists and keeping credentials secure.
Customer will not resell the Services, use them to process data in
violation of law, or attempt to bypass the platform's rate limits or
security controls. Customer grants Provider the right to use Customer's
name and logo in Provider's customer list, unless Customer opts out in
writing.

## Warranties

Provider warrants that the Services will operate in substantial conformity
with the service description under normal use. Provider's sole obligation
for breach of this warranty is to correct the non-conformity or refund the
fees for the affected period. Customer warrants that it has the right to
process the data it uploads and that its use of the Services does not
violate applicable law. To the maximum extent permitted by law, the
Services are provided "as is" except for the warranties stated in this
section.

## Insurance

Each party will maintain, at its own expense, commercial general liability
insurance with limits of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence, and will
provide certificates of insurance upon request. Provider additionally
maintains professional liability (errors and omissions) coverage with
limits of at least $2,000,000, and cyber liability coverage with limits of
at least $5,000,000.

## Notices

Notices under this agreement are given in writing to the addresses in the
order form, or to such other address as a party designates by notice.
Notices are deemed given on the date of delivery for hand delivery, the
date of sending for email, and three business days after mailing for
registered mail. Email notices are effective only where the parties have
designated email addresses for notices in the order form.
