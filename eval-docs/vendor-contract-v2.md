# Master Services Agreement — Acme Corp (v2)

*Agreement ID: MSA-2025-0042. Signed 2025-04-10 — supersedes MSA-2023-0042.*

## Parties

This Master Services Agreement is between Acme Corp ("Customer") and Aurora
Labs Inc. ("Provider") for the provision of the Atlas analytics platform.

## Services

Provider will make available the Atlas analytics platform, including
dashboards, SQL querying, and scheduled report delivery, as described in the
applicable service description. Access is granted to up to 100 named users.

The Services are provided as a hosted, software-as-a-service offering.
Customer may add named users up to the licensed limit without additional
documentation; exceeding the limit requires an order form amendment.
Provider may make changes to the Services during the term, provided the
changes do not materially reduce the functionality described in the
service description. Version 2 doubles the licensed user count from the
50 users in MSA-2023-0042.

## Fees and payment

The annual fee is $60,000, payable quarterly in advance. Fees exclude
applicable taxes. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

Invoices are issued on the first day of each quarter. Customer will pay
within 30 days of the invoice date. Provider may withhold new user
provisioning while an invoice is more than 15 days past due, and may
suspend the Services entirely if payment is more than 30 days past due.
Fees are non-cancellable for the current term and non-refundable except as
set out in the termination section.

## Term and renewal

**Initial term: 24 months (amended 2025).** The agreement renews
automatically for successive 24-month terms unless either party gives
written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the
then-current term.

## Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach with 30 days written notice
and an opportunity to cure. Provider may suspend service for non-payment
after 15 days past due.

Either party may terminate immediately if the other becomes insolvent or
ceases operations. Upon termination for convenience (available after the
first 12 months of the term), Customer receives a pro-rata refund of
prepaid fees for the unused portion of the term. Upon termination for
Provider's uncured breach, Customer receives a refund of prepaid fees and
the remedies in the limitation of liability section apply.

## Limitation of liability

Each party's aggregate liability is limited to the fees paid during the
24 months preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or
consequential damages.

"Consequential damages" includes loss of profits, loss of data, loss of
goodwill, and business interruption, whether foreseeable or not. The
liability cap in Version 2 is based on the 24 months preceding the claim,
extended from the 12-month window in Version 1 to reflect the longer term.

## Data protection

The parties will execute a DPA governing personal data processed under
this agreement. Provider processes Customer Content solely to provide the
Services.

Customer retains all rights in Customer Content. Provider acquires no
rights in Customer Content other than the limited right to process it to
provide the Services. In Version 2, Provider may use de-identified data
for product improvement only where Customer has not opted out in writing;
the opt-out is registered in the admin console under Settings > Privacy.

## Service availability

Provider will provide the Services with a monthly uptime commitment of
99.9% and service credits per the Service Level Agreement in effect at the
time of the outage. Version 2 incorporates the SLA by reference, replacing
the best-efforts availability language and quarterly review in Version 1.

If measured availability falls below the committed level, Customer may
claim credits under the SLA claim process, and the SLA's exclusions apply.

## Support

Provider will provide 24/7 support for SEV1 and SEV2 incidents, and
business-hours support for all other requests, with initial response
targets per the support table in the SLA. Support is provided in English.
Customer's primary and secondary contacts are set out in the order form.

## Security and audit

Provider maintains the security program described in the SOC 2 Type II
report, a copy of which is available to Customer under an NDA. Customer
may request a copy of the most recent SOC 2 report annually. Provider will
notify Customer without undue delay of any security incident affecting
Customer Content, and in no event later than 72 hours after confirmation.

## Confidentiality

Each party will protect the other's Confidential Information using the
same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and in
no event less than reasonable care. Confidential Information includes
Customer Content, pricing, and the terms of this agreement. The
confidentiality obligations survive for 3 years after termination, and
for 5 years for trade secrets.

## Intellectual property

Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property.
Customer owns the dashboards, queries, and reports it creates with the
Services. Provider owns the platform, the software, and any improvements.
Neither party grants the other rights beyond those explicitly granted in
this agreement, except that Customer grants Provider the limited license
needed to host, process, and display Customer Content in the Services.

## Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California.

The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal
courts in San Francisco County, California. This agreement is the entire
agreement between the parties and supersedes prior written or oral
agreements, including MSA-2023-0042. Amendments must be in writing and
signed by both parties.

## Ordering and users

The Services are ordered through order forms issued under this agreement.
Each order form identifies the tier, the number of named users, the fee,
and the service term. Version 2 licenses 100 named users, double the 50
users under MSA-2023-0042. Additional users are added by order form at the
then-current per-user rate. Named users are individuals, not shared
accounts, and the Customer is responsible for the accuracy of its user
list. User seats may be reassigned within the licensed limit without an
order form change.

## Customer obligations

Customer is responsible for the accounts provisioned under the agreement,
including maintaining accurate user lists and keeping credentials secure.
Customer will not resell the Services, use them to process data in
violation of law, or attempt to bypass the platform's rate limits or
security controls. Customer grants Provider the right to use Customer's
name and logo in Provider's customer list, unless Customer opts out in
writing.

## Warranties

Provider warrants that the Services will operate in substantial conformity
with the service description under normal use, and that the security
measures described in the SOC 2 report are maintained during the term.
Provider's sole obligation for breach of the warranty is to correct the
non-conformity or refund the fees for the affected period. Customer
warrants that it has the right to process the data it uploads and that its
use of the Services does not violate applicable law.

## Insurance

Each party will maintain, at its own expense, commercial general liability
insurance with limits of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence, and will
provide certificates of insurance upon request. Provider additionally
maintains professional liability (errors and omissions) coverage with
limits of at least $2,000,000, and cyber liability coverage with limits of
at least $5,000,000.

## Data retention and return

Upon termination or expiration of this agreement, Customer may export its
content for 60 days. Provider will then delete Customer Content within 30
days of the export window closing, and will certify the deletion on
request. Backup copies are purged no later than 30 days after the primary
deletion. This section survives termination. Version 2 adds the deletion
certification, which Version 1 did not require.

## Notices

Notices under this agreement are given in writing to the addresses in the
order form, or to such other address as a party designates by notice.
Notices are deemed given on the date of delivery for hand delivery, the
date of sending for email, and three business days after mailing for
registered mail. Email notices are effective only where the parties have
designated email addresses for notices in the order form.
