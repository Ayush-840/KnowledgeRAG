# Aurora Labs Data Privacy Policy

## Overview

This policy describes how Aurora Labs collects, uses, and protects personal
data when you use our products, websites, and services. Aurora Labs acts as
a data processor for customer workspace content and as a data controller
for account and billing information. This policy applies to all customers
worldwide, with regional sections for the European Union and California.

## What we collect

We collect account information such as your name, work email address, and
organization. When you subscribe, our payment processor collects billing
details including the last four digits of your payment method and your
billing address. We do not store full credit card numbers on our servers.
We also collect usage telemetry such as feature usage, page views, and
error logs, which we use to improve the products.

## How we use personal data

Account data is used to provision your workspace, authenticate users, and
send service and billing notifications. Usage telemetry is aggregated and
used for product improvement; it is never sold to third parties. We use
cookies and similar technologies for authentication, security, and to
remember preferences. Legal bases include performance of a contract,
legitimate interest, and consent where required.

## Data retention

Workspace content is retained for as long as your account is active and for
30 days after account deletion. Backup copies are retained for up to 90
days after deletion, after which they are destroyed. Audit logs are
retained for 90 days on standard plans and up to one year on compliance
plans. Billing records are retained for seven years to satisfy tax
obligations.

## Security measures

We protect personal data using industry-standard controls including
encryption at rest with AES-256, encryption in transit with TLS 1.3, and
role-based access controls. Access to production data is logged and
reviewed quarterly. We maintain a SOC 2 Type II certification and undergo
independent penetration testing twice a year. In the event of a data
breach, we notify affected customers within 72 hours as required by GDPR.

## Subprocessors

We share personal data only with subprocessors that need it to provide the
service. Current subprocessors include Amazon Web Services for hosting,
Stripe for payment processing, and Intercom for support ticketing. Each
subprocessor is bound by a data processing agreement. The full list of
subprocessors is published on our trust page and updated at least 30 days
before a new subprocessor is onboarded.

## GDPR rights

If you are in the European Union or the UK, you have the right to access,
rectify, and erase your personal data, and the right to data portability.
You may also object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of
these rights, email privacy@auroralabs.com, and we will respond within 30
days. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

## California privacy rights

California residents have the right to know what personal data we collect,
the right to delete personal data, and the right to opt out of the sale or
sharing of personal data. We do not sell personal data, and we honor
browser-based opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control.

## Data processing addendum

Enterprise customers can sign our Data Processing Addendum, which covers
sub-processing, international data transfers, and security obligations.
The DPA incorporates the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the
European Commission for transfers outside the EEA.

## International transfers

Data is hosted in the region you select at signup, currently the United
States or the European Union. Transfers between regions are protected by
Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable, the UK Addendum. We
review transfer mechanisms annually and update this policy when the legal
landscape changes.

## Children's privacy

Our services are intended for business use and are not directed to
individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data
from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data,
contact us, and we will delete the information promptly.
