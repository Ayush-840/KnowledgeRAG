Aurora Labs — Product Overview
===============================

About Aurora Labs
-----------------

Aurora Labs is a software company founded in 2019 and headquartered in
Portland, Oregon. The company employs 400 people and serves customers in 40
countries. Its mission is to make real-time data analytics accessible to
teams of every size, from two-person startups to global enterprises with
thousands of seats.

The company is bootstrapped and profitable, with annual recurring revenue
growing more than 60 percent year over year. Aurora Labs raised a Series A
in 2021 led by Northgate Partners and a Series B in 2023 led by Meridian
Capital, but the founding team retains majority control and makes product
decisions with a long-term horizon. The company operates fully remote with
engineering hubs in Portland, Lisbon, and Bangalore.

Products
--------

Atlas is the company's flagship analytics platform. Atlas costs $49 per user
per month and includes real-time dashboards, custom reporting, and a 14-day
free trial. It is designed for product and marketing teams that need to turn
raw event data into decisions without writing SQL. Atlas connects to web
analytics, product analytics, and advertising platforms out of the box, and
its drag-and-drop report builder ships with more than 40 chart types.

Atlas Pro is the mid-tier plan. Atlas Pro costs $99 per user per month and
adds single sign-on, audit logs, and priority support. It is intended for
growth-stage companies that need compliance-ready access controls and
permission groups. Atlas Pro also unlocks versioned dashboards, scheduled
email exports, and a 90-day data retention window on the standard plan.

Atlas Enterprise is the top tier. It uses custom pricing based on the number
of seats and includes a dedicated support engineer, custom data retention
contracts, and deployment in the customer's own cloud account. Enterprise
accounts can negotiate annual contracts with volume discounts and receive a
99.95 percent uptime service-level agreement backed by service credits.

Beacon is a lightweight alerting service. Beacon costs $19 per user per month
and integrates directly with Slack and Microsoft Teams. Alerts can be routed
by severity, owner, and schedule, and the alert deduplication engine collapses
related incidents into a single thread. Beacon supports email, webhook, and
SMS delivery channels and can page on-call engineers through PagerDuty.

Beacon Plus is the advanced alerting plan. Beacon Plus costs $39 per user per
month and adds on-call scheduling, escalation policies, and a public status
page. It is aimed at SRE and DevOps teams that run production systems around
the clock. Beacon Plus includes a 30-day alert history and unlimited
integrations.

Nimbus is the company's managed data pipeline product. Nimbus uses custom
pricing based on data volume and offers a 99.9% uptime service-level
agreement. It connects to over 60 data sources out of the box, including
databases, data warehouses, and third-party SaaS APIs, and can stream
millions of events per minute into a customer's analytics store.

Nimbus Lite is the entry-level pipeline plan. Nimbus Lite costs $29 per month
for up to 100,000 events per month and includes five data sources and a
14-day data retention window. It is designed for developers who want to try
streaming pipelines without a sales conversation.

Nimbus Enterprise adds private networking, field-level data masking, and a
99.99 percent uptime commitment. It targets regulated industries such as
financial services and healthcare that need guaranteed delivery and
compliance controls. Nimbus Enterprise includes a named solutions architect
and a 24-hour support SLA.

Pricing and Plans
-----------------

All Aurora Labs products are billed monthly and can be cancelled at any time.
Annual billing is available at a 20 percent discount, and all paid plans
include unlimited dashboards, unlimited API calls on the standard tier, and
email support. Trials do not require a credit card, and trial accounts can
convert to paid plans with a single click at the end of the 14-day period.

Volume pricing starts at 50 seats, and enterprise contracts are quoted on an
annual basis. Aurora Labs offers non-profit and education discounts of 30
percent, and startup credits of up to $1,000 for companies that have raised
less than $5 million. All prices are listed in US dollars; international
customers are billed in their local currency where supported.

Security and Compliance
-----------------------

Aurora Labs holds a SOC 2 Type II certification and encrypts all customer
data at rest with AES-256. The platform is fully GDPR compliant and supports
single sign-on through SAML and OAuth. Data in transit is protected with TLS
1.3, and every request is authenticated with short-lived tokens. The
security team runs quarterly penetration tests and maintains a public
security.txt endpoint and a responsible disclosure program with a 90-day
timeline for patching reported vulnerabilities.

Aurora Labs maintains a formal incident response plan and notifies affected
customers within 24 hours of a confirmed security incident. Backups are
encrypted and stored in a separate region, with a recovery point objective of
15 minutes and a recovery time objective of 4 hours. The company's data
processing addendum is available on request, and subprocessors are listed in
the privacy policy.

Support
-------

All customers receive 24/7 support with a one-hour response time on the
enterprise plan. A community forum and public documentation are available
free of charge. Standard plans receive support through a ticketing portal
with a four-hour response target during business hours, and Beacon Plus and
Atlas Pro customers get priority queue placement and a two-hour target.
Enterprise customers are assigned a dedicated support engineer who joins
their Slack workspace.

Onboarding and Migration
------------------------

Aurora Labs offers a guided onboarding program for new customers, including a
free implementation workshop for Atlas Enterprise and Nimbus Enterprise
accounts. Migration tooling can import dashboards, alerts, and historical
data from legacy analytics platforms, and the company publishes a detailed
migration guide covering schema mapping and downtime planning. Most
customers complete their migration in under two weeks.

Careers
-------

Aurora Labs is hiring across engineering, product, and go-to-market teams.
The company publishes a transparent salary band for every role and offers
four weeks of paid time off, a home office stipend, and a learning budget of
$2,000 per year. Interview loops include a paid take-home exercise and are
designed to finish within two weeks of the first conversation.

Roadmap
-------

The 2026 roadmap includes an AI copilot that answers questions about your
dashboards in natural language, a mobile app for Beacon alerting, and
expanded warehouse-native integrations. Aurora Labs also plans to ship
on-premises editions of Atlas and Nimbus for regulated customers that cannot
use the public cloud. Feature requests are tracked publicly, and the
roadmap is updated monthly.

Common Questions
----------------

Do I need a credit card to try Atlas? No — the 14-day trial requires only a
work email address. Can I switch plans mid-cycle? Yes, upgrades take effect
immediately and downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle. Is my data
portable? Yes, all plans can export raw data and dashboard definitions at
any time, and the standard API exposes the full data model.
